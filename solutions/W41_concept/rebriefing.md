# Rebriefing — draft v1

**Task (in our words):** The "Americans on AI" survey turns how the US public feels about AI into percentages every two weeks.
We design a way into this data that lets a non-expert *experience* where public opinion stands — and where they stand themselves —
delivered as an animatic, branched animatic or interactive prototype, for the web or an exhibition.

**Client vs. end users:** Client = Interactive Things (builds the official platform; contact Gerhard Bliedung).
Data owner = Athena Insights. End users = people who are *not* AI or data experts (see target groups). The lecturers are reviewers, not users.

**Target groups, prioritised**
1. *Primary:* young adults 20–35 who use AI regularly but have no settled opinion — want to compare themselves with others.
2. *Secondary:* people who barely use AI and are mostly worried — want to know if they're alone with it.
3. *Not our focus:* researchers / journalists (they need the raw data platform, which Interactive Things is building).

**Success criterion (for the client):** Visitors can find their own group, read the main finding correctly and name one surprise —
without help (tested in the W48 user test; see criteria).

**Non-negotiable guard rails**
- *Attitude to the data:* show sentiment neutrally — no "AI is good/bad" framing; the survey itself is designed to be neutral.
- *Honesty:* "No answer" / "Not sure" stay visible; the pilot wave (other method) is marked or left out; differences smaller than the margin of error (±3–4 pts) are not presented as change.
- *Currency:* a new wave arrives every two weeks → the story must not depend on a fixed date or a specific number ("two in three", not "68.8 %").
- *Technical:* web-based or exhibition prototype; data from the official CSV/JSON.

**Data basis:** Americans on AI, 7 waves (12 Jun – 10 Sep 2026), n ≈ 1,000–2,400 per wave, 6 questions / 20 items, 12 subgroups.
Updates every 2 weeks via the same structure.

**Collaboration:** Feedback from Alain (concept/UX), Gordan (feasibility), Fiona (data) in class; Interactive Things at the final presentation.
Decisions within the group: by the group; in doubt: the lecturer of that week. *(confirm)*

**Out of scope:** a full data platform or dashboard; data from other countries; our own survey; production-ready code.
