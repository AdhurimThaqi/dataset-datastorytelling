How to read this data

U.S. adults 18+, fielded by NORC on the probability-based AmeriSpeak panel, about 1,000–2,400 people per wave, weighted to Census benchmarks. Margin of error ±2.5–4.2 points per wave, larger for subgroups — small differences between groups or waves can be noise. "Not sure" and "No answer" are real answers (up to ~29 % on some items) and are not hidden here. The 12 Jun pilot used a non-probability online panel without a "No answer" option and is only shown for transparency.
