How to spot AI (rules of thumb)

  • Too perfect. Light that's lovely everywhere at once, glassy water, no haze, no clutter.
  • Zoom into the details. Trees, rocks and far-away houses that melt into mush or repeat.
  • No traces of people. Real valleys have paths, walls, fences, signs and power lines.
  • Texts: smooth but empty. “Perfect blend”, “truly unforgettable”, advice that fits any place. People add names, numbers and odd details.

Each new AI model fixes some of these. That's why so many people already find it hard.
