# Basic verbal concept (words only, ≤ half a page)

1. **Who is it for?** (one target group, from W40)
2. **What should this person see, know or do differently afterwards?**
3. **Key message (one sentence, without judging the data):**
4. **Points of connection** — what should this person latch on to?
5. **Who leads:** author / audience / alternating — and why?
6. **What does someone get who doesn't click anything?**
7. **How will we know it worked?** goal → signal → metric
8. **Context:** web or exhibition?
9. **Data basis & currency:** which indicators; how it stays valid when new waves arrive
10. **Scope of the prototype:** what gets built, what is only described
11. **Risks & open questions** (incl. assumptions nobody has objected to yet)

Test: read it to someone from another group. If they have to ask what you want, it isn't finished.
Every decision must trace back to the rebriefing or the user survey — otherwise label it as an assumption or delete it.

- Why? ↑
- **Problem statement:** e.g. "We show how concern about AI changes over time."
