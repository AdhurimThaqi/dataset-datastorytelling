1_master: complete dataset with all waves and subgroups
2_waves: one CSV per wave with all subgroups
3_subgroups: one CSV per subgroup across all waves
4_wave_subgroups: one folder per wave containing the subgroup CSVs for that wave
5_own_export: Under Chapter "4. Exports" in the Jupyter Notebook you will find an export code cell where you can filter the data through your chosen waves, supergroups or topics to create your own CSV file.
