## The project
Make the **"Americans on AI"** survey (Athena Insights, fielded by NORC) experienceable — for **web or an exhibition**.
Format: **animatic, branched animatic or interactive prototype** + a short justification of your decisions.
Client / audience: **Interactive Things** (Zurich) — final presentation **17 Dec 2026**.
Groups of 2–3. One shared Google Doc per group with all lecturers (weekly log + research).
