# Prompt used for the AI draft (direction A)

- **Optional W42 step (§9):** "use AI only after sketching". The draft was made **after** `wireframes/A.svg`. It uses the wireframe as "my sketch".
  → When the hand sketch of A exists, re-run the prompt with the photo (`photos/A.jpg`) and compare.
- **Tool:** Claude (Claude Code), 20.10.2026.
- **Output:** `index.html`, generated by `build.py` so that every number is read from the CSV at build time.
- **Template:** W42 script §9.2, filled in. Interview content is paraphrased, not quoted (check 3).

---

```text
I am a design student. Please build a rough first prototype of a data story.

MY IDEA
The data shows that about 2 in 3 US adults are concerned about AI (ai_sentiment) and about 2 in 3 say
"AI is coming into our lives whether we want it or not" (ai_agency); younger adults choose
"people like me can shape how much AI is part of our lives" more often than older adults.
People told us, in our own words: an older interviewee felt left out of the decision about AI;
a younger one could not tell whether a video online was real.
Our question: How might we let young adults find out whether their feeling about AI is "normal",
and show them that being worried and thinking "it's coming anyway" are both majority views?
This direction lets young adults in Switzerland (web, phone first) experience where their own answer
sits among 1,000 US adults, by age group.

MY SKETCH
The image shows my sketch (wireframes/A.svg, 6 phone frames). Follow its layout and order:
1 guess real or AI · 2 reveal "4 in 5 see people struggling with real vs fake" · 3 answer the sentiment
question yourself · 4 your dot among 1,000 dots, then by age · 5 the agency question, dots re-sort,
age comparison · 6 one closing sentence. If nobody clicks for 8 s, play 1 → 2 → 5 → 6 by itself.
Do not turn it into a standard chart.

MY DATA
The file survey_all_waves_all_subgroups.csv has these columns:
Subgroup: total, 4 age bands, gender, party, race/ethnicity · Wave: start date of the wave ·
Question_ID: ai_sentiment, ai_agency, ai_community_impact, … · Full_Question: wording ·
Dimension: item text for multi-item questions · Answer: answer code · Percentage: weighted %.
Use only the latest wave (2026-09-10). The pilot wave 2026-06-12 is excluded.
Only use numbers from this file. Do not invent, round or fill in numbers.
Do not average the answers; show how they are spread. Keep "none of these", "neither", "not sure"
and "no answer" visible as their own categories.

MAPPING
- one dot → about 1 in 1,000 US adults (Percentage × 10, sum kept at 1,000)
- Answer → position in the dot field (very excited → very concerned → none → no answer)
- Answer → colour: excited = blue (two steps), concerned = orange (two steps), none/neither/no answer = greys.
  No red/green, no good/bad framing.
- the visitor's answer → one extra outlined dot inside its answer block
- Subgroup (age band) → the same dots re-coloured with that group's distribution
- Question (ai_agency instead of ai_sentiment) → the same dots re-sorted; they are not the same people
- Party and race only behind a toggle, never by default.

EXAMPLE
From our own course site (index.html / w41.html), use the colours of the surfaces, the fonts and the
calm layout, not a chart type.

RESULT
One HTML file that opens in a browser. Rough is fine.

AT THE END, LIST
1. Every mapping you used.
2. Every change you made to the data (filtering, grouping, averaging, rounding).
3. Everything you had to guess.
```
