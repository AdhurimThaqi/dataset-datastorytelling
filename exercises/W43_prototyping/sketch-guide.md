# Sketch guide: what to draw on paper (~30 min)

**The milestone is the hand sketch.** The SVGs in `wireframes/` are only a reference.

**Material:**
- 3 × A4, landscape, folded into 6 boxes (fold in half, then in thirds)
- black marker, plus one grey pen for notes
- no ruler, no colour

**Rules from the W42 script:**
- Don't erase anything.
- Write what you notice next to the box, in a few words.
- Keep the failed tries too.

**Per box, always write:**
- frame number + title on top
- `leads: author / visitor` in a corner
- the one number shown in that frame, rounded ("2 in 3"), with "US adults" next to it

---

## A · Real or AI? (web) — ~10 min
Draw a phone outline in each box.
1. **Hook:** a big empty rectangle with a "?" in it (the image), and two buttons, `REAL` and `AI`. Note: "no score".
2. **Reveal:** a large "4 in 5", with a short line underneath: "see people struggle real vs fake". Small: "not sure 7".
3. **You answer:** the question at the top, then 4 stacked buttons + a 5th dashed one, "none of these".
4. **You vs. America:** a block of dots (just hatch ~10 rows of dots, don't count to 1,000). Circle one dot thickly: "you".
   Underneath, a row of 4 tabs: `18–34 | 35–49 | 50–64 | 65+`.
5. **The turn:** the same block of dots, split into two piles: a small "can shape 17" and a big "coming anyway 68".
   Two small boxes: "18–34: 27" and "65+: 8".
6. **Close:** 3 lines of scribbled text (the key message) + a small box "US adults · latest wave".
   - Arrow note in the margin: "click nothing → 1, 2, 5, 6 play by themselves".

## B · Stand where you feel (exhibition) — ~10 min
Draw the room **from above** (a rectangle = floor) in boxes 1, 2, 4 and 5. Draw a **wall from the front** in boxes 3 and 6.
1. **Attract:** the floor rectangle, a line across it marked `very exc · somewhat exc · somewhat conc · very conc`.
   A separate square of the same size: "none of these". Scattered dots.
2. **Step on:** a stick figure (top view = a circle) on "somewhat concerned". Dot piles under each spot.
   Write the pile sizes: 47 · 167 · 348 · 339 · none 91.
3. **Wall:** "348 of 1,000 stand here with you". Add a second figure on a different spot (group visit).
4. **Age lanes:** the floor split into 4 strips: 18–34, 35–49, 50–64, 65+. Write the concerned share: 61, 66, 74, 76.
   - Note: "18–34 vs 35–49 = about the same".
5. **Flip:** a new floor line: `can shape ←→ coming anyway`, with piles 174 | 675 | neither 140.
6. **Two walls:** left wall = dots "US adults", right wall = tally marks "this room today, not representative".
   - Margin note: "passer-by sees 1, 2, 4, 5 on loop".

## C · Dinner table (branched animatic) — ~10 min
Draw an oval table with 4 circles (seats) + 1 dashed circle ("you") in each box. **Draw the branch as arrows on the sheet.**
1. **Table:** label the seats 18–34, 35–49, 50–64, 65+. A card on the table: "a video is going around: real?"
2. **Shared moment:** a speech bubble from every seat: "~4 in 5 can't tell".
3. **Choice:** two buttons at the bottom: "sit next to 18–34" / "sit next to 65+". Write "8 s → default".
   - Draw **two arrows** out of this box.
4. **4a (18–34):** shade the 18–34 seat. Bubble: "61 worried · 25 excited · 27 can shape".
5. **4b (65+):** shade the 65+ seat. Bubble: "76 worried · 16 excited · 8 can shape".
6. **Merge:** both arrows meet here. The two shaded seats face each other, with "27 vs 8" between them.
   The host's bubble: the key message.
   - Margin note: "fictional people, real numbers".
