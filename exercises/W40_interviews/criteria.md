# Success criteria (revised after 3 interviews)

Form: **"Someone from [our target group] can [notice / understand / do something] — without help."**

| # | Criterion | Source quote (interview #) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Someone from our target group can say whether "people like them" are more or less worried than average — without help. | "With colleagues we joke about it. Nobody really says they're scared." (02) |
| 2 | Someone from our target group can find a moment in the story that matches their own experience with AI — without help. | "There was this video of a politician on TikTok … I still don't know." (01) · "the Pope in a white jacket and we all believed it" (03) |
| 3 | Someone from our target group can repeat the main finding ("most are worried and think it's coming anyway") in their own words — without help. | "If I could say something smart about it at dinner with my family" (01) |
| ★ Surprise | Someone from our target group notices that younger people feel more able to shape AI than older people — without help. | "On the news they say it will change everything. Nobody asks us if we want that." (03) |
