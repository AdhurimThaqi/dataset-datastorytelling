# Success criteria (revised after 3 interviews, October 2026)

Form: **"Someone from [our target group] can [notice / understand / do something] — without help."**
Rule: no quote, no criterion. Each one can be checked in the W48 user test.

| # | Criterion | Source quotes (interview #) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Someone from our target group can say whether "people like them" (same age group) are more or less worried about AI than the average — without help. | "If it showed what other people my age think about AI" (01) · "compare my own views with those of people in different professions" (02) · "a simple overview showing what different age groups think" (03) |
| 2 | Someone from our target group can find a moment in the story that matches their own experience with AI — without help. | "I wasn't completely sure, so I didn't share it." (01) · "I searched for more information before believing it." (02) · "I couldn't tell whether it was genuine, and I didn't know how to check it." (03) |
| 3 | Someone from our target group can retell the main finding in their own words, without technical terms — without help. | "It should explain things simply, without using too many technical words." (03) · "I would want to see credible data" (02) |
| ★ Surprise | Someone from our target group notices that these numbers come from a representative survey of US adults, unlike comment sections and polls — without help. | "The challenge is knowing whether the people commenting represent the wider population or just a very vocal group." (02) · "Reddit, YouTube comments, or Instagram polls" (01) · "I don't normally look for research studies." (03) |

Guard rail confirmed by the interviews (not a criterion): "I wouldn't want something that only promotes AI or only focuses on its risks." (02) → R11, neutral towards AI.
