# Interview 03 — Retail worker, 34, rarely uses AI

- **Who:** P3, man, 34, retail worker. Uses AI only occasionally, mostly meets it through social media and online services; uncertain about it.
- **Why this person:** contrast case: rare user (the majority of US adults use AI rarely, see desk research S8). *Planned was someone 50+; not reached yet.*
- **Date:** October 2026 · **Interviewer:** Adhurim Thaqi (solo project, notes written up after the interview)

## Answers (as given)

**Broad openers**
1. *Last time you came across AI?* — "A few days ago, I saw a video on Facebook that looked very strange. Someone in the comments said it was made with AI. I hadn't thought much about it before, but it made me wonder how many videos online are fake."
2. *What do you use AI for in a normal week?* — "I don't really use ChatGPT regularly. I mostly use my phone, Google, and social media. I probably interact with AI without realizing it, such as when an app recommends videos or when my phone suggests words while I'm typing."
3. *A moment when AI surprised you?* — "I saw a video of a person speaking and moving in a way that looked completely real. Someone explained that it had been generated by AI. I was surprised because I thought I would be able to recognize something fake more easily."

**Deeper**

4. *Last time you weren't sure if something was real or AI?* — "It was a video shared by someone I know. I couldn't tell whether it was genuine, and I didn't know how to check it. I looked at the comments, but people were arguing about it, so I eventually moved on."
5. *How has AI changed your work or everyday life?* — "I haven't noticed a major change in my daily work. Sometimes I hear that companies are introducing AI, and I wonder whether it will affect people's jobs. I don't know enough about it to say what will happen, but I would like to understand it better."
6. *Last AI news story that stuck with you?* — "I remember hearing that AI can create realistic pictures and videos of people doing things they never actually did. I mentioned it to my brother because I thought it could cause problems if people believed everything they saw online."
7. *How do you talk about AI with friends or family?* — "We usually talk about it when someone shares a strange video or an article. Some people think AI is impressive, while others think it will cause problems. Most of us don't really know how it works, so the conversation is usually based on what we have seen online."
8. *Last time you decided not to use AI?* — "I needed information about a product recently, and I used Google instead of ChatGPT. I'm used to searching for websites and reading the information myself. I didn't really see a reason to use another tool for something simple."

**Success**

9. *How would you notice a website/exhibit about AI was worth your time?* — "I would understand AI better and know how to recognize fake videos or pictures. It should explain things simply, without using too many technical words. I would also like to know what AI can and cannot do."
10. *Where do you go to find out what people in general think?* — "I usually look at Facebook comments, YouTube, or ask people I know. I don't normally look for research studies. It would be useful to see a simple overview showing what different age groups think and why."

## Key quotes → what it means for us
| Quote | Insight |
|---|---|
| Q3: "I thought I would be able to recognize something fake more easily." | **S1:** even a rare user meets fakes, and overestimated himself. |
| Q4: "I couldn't tell whether it was genuine, and I didn't know how to check it." | **S3:** rare users lack the tools to check; AI happens *to* them. |
| Q5: "I don't know enough about it to say what will happen" | **S2:** uncertainty rather than fear. |
| Q7: "the conversation is usually based on what we have seen online" | **S4 / S7:** opinions come from what circulates online. |
| Q9: "It should explain things simply, without using too many technical words." | **Criterion 3:** plain language, no statistics jargon. |
| Q10: "a simple overview showing what different age groups think and why" | **Criterion 1:** comparison by age, said by a rare user without prompting. |

**Surprise:** P3 most wants to learn *how to spot fakes*. Our dataset can show that most people struggle with it, but it can't teach detection → name this limit in the close.
