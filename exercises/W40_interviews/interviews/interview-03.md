# Interview 03

- **Who:** P3, family member, ~58, works in retail, rarely uses AI knowingly
- **Why this person:** contrast case / secondary target group (R7): the most concerned age group in the data
- **Date / place / duration:** [20.10.2026] · at home · ~20 min
- **Interviewer / note-taker:** [Adhurim] / [Thaqi]

| What was said (verbatim quote) | What we observed / context | What it means for us (insight) |
|---|---|---|
| Q1: "I don't use it. Well, maybe I do and don't know it. The phone does things now that I didn't ask for." | Had to think for a while. | AI is experienced as something that happens *to* them, not something they choose. **Supports H3** (less sense of agency). |
| Q4: "My friend sent a picture in the WhatsApp group of the Pope in a white jacket and we all believed it." | Animated, told the story in detail. | **Supports H1:** real vs. fake is the shared experience across ages. A strong opening hook for both target groups. |
| Q6: "On the news they say it will change everything. Nobody asks us if we want that." | Raised voice slightly. | **Supports H2:** worry + powerlessness. Clear "inevitable" framing. |
| Q7: "My kids explain it to me. I trust them more than the internet." | | People talk about AI across generations. The secondary group learns through the primary group. |
| Q9: "If it's not too complicated. If I need to click a lot I'll stop." | | The click-nothing path is important for this group (concept §4). |
| Q10: "The news, SRF. Or I ask around." | | Swiss sources. **Supports H4:** US numbers need a clear "this is the US" label and a bridge to the visitor. |

**Surprise:** P3 was less afraid than expected and more annoyed at not being asked. The feeling is about being left out of the decision, not fear of the technology. This could make "can society still shape it?" (ai_agency) more important than the sentiment question.

**Possible criterion:** Someone from our target group can repeat the main finding ("most people are worried and think it's coming anyway") in their own words — without help.
