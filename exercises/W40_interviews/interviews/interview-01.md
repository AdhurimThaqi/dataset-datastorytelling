**Surprise:** P1 did not describe themselves as worried at all, but described themselves as more "used to it". The excited ↔ concerned scale may not fit people like P1. "None of these" could be their real answer.

**Possible criterion:** Someone from our target group can find a moment in the story that matches their own experience with AI (e.g. a fake video) — without help.
