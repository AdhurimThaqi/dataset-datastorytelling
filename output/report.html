Data Story Examples

I.BA_DAS practice - 4 real datasets, each charted with a headline title that states the insight.

Gapminder

Classic Hans Rosling dataset. Good for an animated story: time is the narrative axis.

CO2 emissions

Our World in Data. Good for ethics: the same numbers tell opposite stories depending on framing (total vs per person, produced vs consumed).

Titanic

Small and personal. Good for a branching animatic: every passenger attribute is a decision node.

Seattle weather

Daily time series. Good for practising cleaning & smoothing and seasonal patterns.
